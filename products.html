Our Copper Sulphate Product Range

Three physical forms. Six application-specific grades. One consistent standard of purity.

Physical Forms

Choose the Form That Suits Your Process

Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate is the base compound behind every grade we manufacture — supplied as coarse crystal or finely milled powder depending on how you plan to use it.

Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate

CuSO₄·5H₂O — the standard hydrated compound, ~25% copper by weight, bright blue in colour.

  • Base form for all grades
  • 25 kg & 50 kg bags
  • Water soluble
View Details
Copper Sulphate Crystal

Coarse, irregular blue crystals — the traditional form used for pond dosing and Bordeaux mixture.

  • Slower, controlled dissolution
  • Low dust generation
  • Ideal for manual dosing
View Details
Copper Sulphate Powder

Finely ground powder for rapid, uniform dissolution in feed premixes and spray tanks.

  • Fast dissolution
  • Uniform blending
  • Custom mesh sizes
View Details
