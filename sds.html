Hazard Summary

Harmful if swallowed (H302). Causes serious eye irritation (H319). Causes skin irritation (H315). May cause respiratory irritation (H335). Very toxic to aquatic life with long-lasting effects (H410).

Recommended PPE

Chemical-resistant gloves, safety goggles or face shield, and a dust mask/respirator when handling powder forms. Wash hands thoroughly after handling and before eating or drinking.
