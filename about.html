About CopperSulphate.net

Manufacturing and supplying high-purity Copper Sulphate since our founding — built on chemistry expertise, quality control, and dependable supply.

Our Story

From Raw Copper to Reliable Chemistry

CopperSulphate.net is a specialist manufacturer of Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate (CuSO₄·5H₂O), producing crystal and powder forms across six application-specific grades — Agriculture, Feed, Technical, Electroplating, Laboratory and Industrial.

We built our business on a simple idea: Copper Sulphate is not one product but a family of related products, each with its own purity requirements, particle size and end-use. Rather than selling a single generic grade, we manufacture and package to the specification that each industry actually needs — from analytical-grade reagent for a laboratory to bulk agriculture-grade crystal for a fertilizer blender.

Our Mission

To manufacture consistent, high-purity Copper Sulphate and make the right grade — in the right form, packaging and quantity — easy for every customer to source, whether they need 1 kg for a classroom or a full container for export.

Our Vision
