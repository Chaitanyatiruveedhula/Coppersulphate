Overview

Finely Ground Powder for Rapid Dissolution

Copper Sulphate Powder is produced by milling copper sulphate pentahydrate crystals to a fine, uniform particle size. The increased surface area allows it to dissolve rapidly and blend evenly — properties that matter most in feed premixes, foliar sprays, and fertilizer formulations where uniform copper distribution is critical.

We offer multiple mesh sizes to match your mixing equipment and blending process, from coarse-milled powder for general use to fine, near-micronized powder for premix and formulation work.
