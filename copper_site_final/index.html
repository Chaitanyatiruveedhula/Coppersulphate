Who We Are

A Dependable Source of Copper Sulphate

CopperSulphate.net manufactures and supplies high-purity Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate (CuSO₄·5H₂O) in crystal and powder forms to agriculture, aquaculture, electroplating, water treatment, animal feed and laboratory industries. Every batch is produced under controlled conditions and tested for copper content, purity and impurity levels before dispatch, so customers receive a consistent product order after order.

Whether you need a single 25 kg bag for a laboratory or a full truckload for an agrochemical formulation plant, our team structures packaging, documentation and logistics around your requirement.

Applications

Trusted Across Industries

Agriculture

Fungicide (Bordeaux Mixture), micronutrient fertilizer and foliar spray for copper-deficient soils.

Aquaculture & Water Treatment

Algae and parasite control in fish ponds, reservoirs and industrial cooling water systems.

Electroplating

High-purity copper sulphate solutions for copper plating, PCB manufacturing and electro-refining.

Animal Feed

Trace-mineral copper supplementation for poultry, swine and cattle feed premixes.

Laboratory

Analytical-grade reagent for titrations, Fehling's/Benedict's solutions and classroom demonstrations.

Industrial & Mining

Froth flotation activator, textile mordant, leather processing and wood preservation.
